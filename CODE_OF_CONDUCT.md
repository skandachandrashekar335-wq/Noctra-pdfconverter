# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as
contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project
and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
sexual orientation, religion, or language.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, safe,
and welcoming community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our
community:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces managed by the project, including
GitHub repositories, issue trackers, chat rooms, mailing lists, and video
conferences.

It also applies to individual representation when an individual is acting
in a professional capacity within the project's space.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported by opening an issue with the Security Labs label, or by contacting
the project maintainers at the private reporting path below.

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits,
code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of
Conduct, or to temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for other
behaviors they deem inappropriate, threatening, or silencing.

## Reporting

**Private reporting path**: Please report security-related issues
directly via GitHub Security Advisories. For non-security concerns, open an
issue with the "question" label.

Maintainers will review all reports and investigate accordingly, invoking
the project's incident response process if warranted.
